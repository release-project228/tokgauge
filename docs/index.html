tokgauge

TikTok · analytics · profiles · growth · engagement

tokgauge is an analytics tool for public TikTok accounts. It shows profile statistics, video counts, comments and follower-growth trends so you can understand how an account is doing without needing access to the account itself.

The tool is built for creators who track their own growth, for marketers who research influencers and for viewers who want to see how a trending account evolved. All numbers come from public data, so there is no login and no special permission required.

  • Profile stats such as followers and likes
  • Video counts and recent activity
  • Comments and engagement signals
  • Follower-growth trends over time

The idea is to answer practical questions: is this account growing or stalling, which content gets a reaction and how does one creator compare with another in the same niche. The pages are lightweight, so checking several accounts in a row takes seconds.

The full product runs at https://tokgauge.com.
